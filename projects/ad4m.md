Agent-centric framework for social apps on Holochain: each person runs an executor that holds their did:key identity, a semantic graph of signed Expressions, and pluggable Languages for storing and sharing them. Groups are Neighbourhoods, shared graphs that several apps can join. Apps on it include Flux.
