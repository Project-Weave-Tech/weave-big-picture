A draft standard for the personal context that AI assistants and agents draw on: memory, preferences, directives, owned by the person. Apps and agents read it only under grants the person approves, for a stated purpose and a limited time, with every access logged. Version 2, draft 3, published 6 November 2026; the wire format is still changing. Specification text CC BY 4.0, tools Apache-2.0, from the Loyal Agents initiative.

## Who it is for

People who use AI agents and want to govern what those agents know about them; agents and apps that need that context.

## Connections

Presented at the interopathon. The Trust Tasks Task Force of the ToIP / DIF Decentralized Trust Graph Working Group discusses how it could become one or more trust tasks.
