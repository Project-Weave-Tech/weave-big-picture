An economic planner for groups whose members already trust each other: a mutual aid pod, a co-op, a family, a block. People say what they have and what they need, and a planner over ValueFlows data finds the allocation of labour that meets every need with the least work. Written in Guile Scheme on Spritely Goblins, with KERI identities, a gateway as trust root, and mesh networking over Reticulum.

## Who it is for

Mutual aid pods, cooperatives, neighbourhoods, families: "people you already trust".
